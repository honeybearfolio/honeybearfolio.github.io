Your Finances,
Your Control

A local-first personal finance app for the desktop. Accounts, investments, and net worth stay in a SQLite file on your machine.

Download Free View on GitHub Buy Me a Coffee

Everything You Need

Accounts, investments, assets, and debts, with the charts and tools to see how they add up.

Privacy First, Always

Accounts, investments, and net worth stay in a SQLite file on your machine. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to create.

hard_disk 100% Local cloud_off No Cloud terminal Open Source

Dashboard

Arrange cards for net worth, income against expenses, spending by category, how assets are split, and cash flow. Hide accounts, and give any card its own date range and filters.

bar_chart Charts dashboard_customize Arrange filter_alt Filters

Investments

Holdings, cost basis, and gain or loss. Quotes and daily prices come from Yahoo Finance and feed the allocation chart and the performance heatmap.

Transactions

Cash accounts, transfers, and brokerage trades. A buy or sell records the share leg and the matching cash leg.

Rules & Schedules

Recurring items wait until you apply them. A rule matches a payee by text or by regular expression and fills in the fields you choose.

Assets & Liabilities

A value history for a home, a car, or anything else you own, and a balance history for mortgages, loans, and other debts. Both count in net worth.

Goals

A target funded by the accounts and investments you link to it. See what is reached, on track, behind, or projected.

FIRE Calculator

Starts from your balances, spending, and savings, then projects a target with a single path and a Monte Carlo range.

Import & Export

Bring in CSV, Excel, or JSON. Map the columns, preview the rows, and export JSON, CSV, Excel, or a PDF report for a period you choose.

Multi-Currency

Accounts and transactions can use different currencies. Rates come from Yahoo Finance, and any rate can be replaced with one you enter.

Local Assistant

An optional assistant uses Ollama on your computer. It can look up accounts, transactions, holdings, assets, liabilities, and goals.

Day to day

  • Keep more than one database. Create a file, open an existing one, and switch from the sidebar.
  • Privacy mode masks amounts and leaves the currency symbol visible.
  • The interface is available in English and Spanish.
  • Choose light, dark, high contrast, ink, or follow the system, and scale the type size.
